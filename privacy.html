FokusUnit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the FokusUnit Discord application ("FokusUnit", "the Bot", or "the Application") may process information when it is installed or used in a Discord server.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on how the Application is configured and used, FokusUnit may process limited information necessary for its functionality.

This may include:

  • Discord server IDs.
  • Discord channel IDs.
  • Discord user IDs when required for application functionality or administration.
  • Configuration information related to the Application.
  • Information necessary to create or publish configured announcements.
  • Technical information required for the operation, security, and troubleshooting of the Application.

FokusUnit does not intentionally request sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment-card information, or authentication credentials.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by FokusUnit may be used to:

  • Provide the Application's functionality.
  • Publish configured announcements or content.
  • Maintain application configuration.
  • Troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the Application.
  • Prevent misuse of the Application.

Information is not used for purposes unrelated to the operation of the Application unless otherwise disclosed.

3. Discord Information

FokusUnit operates through Discord's platform.

When the Application interacts with Discord, Discord may independently process information according to Discord's own Privacy Policy.

FokusUnit does not control Discord's processing of information.

4. Data Sharing

FokusUnit does not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate or maintain the Application.
  • Comply with applicable law or a valid legal request.
  • Protect the security, rights, or property of the Application or its users.
  • Address abuse, fraud, or security incidents.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Application's functionality, maintain configuration, resolve technical issues, or comply with applicable obligations.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, it may be deleted.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the Application against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. User and Server Administrator Requests

Server administrators may remove FokusUnit from their Discord server at any time.

If you have a question about information associated with the Application or wish to request deletion of information processed by FokusUnit, you may contact the administrator or developer responsible for the Application through the official support channel associated with FokusUnit.

8. Children's Privacy

FokusUnit does not intentionally collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to the operation of the Application.

Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws when using Discord and FokusUnit.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date will be updated accordingly.

10. Contact

For questions, privacy requests, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, contact the administrator or developer responsible for the FokusUnit application through the official support channel associated with FokusUnit.
